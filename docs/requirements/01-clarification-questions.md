# Clarification Questions: ClaimsConnect

| ID | Question | Why it matters (design impact) | Priority | Ask whom | Default assumption if unanswered |
|----|----------|--------------------------------|----------|----------|----------------------------------|
| Q1 | What does "fair" assignment mean: equal claim count, equal workload by claim value, or round-robin? And what defines "expertise": claim type, region, claim value, or something else? | Decides whether assignment is a simple flow or needs Apex plus a workload field on the adjuster | Blocker | Dan | Round-robin within a region, filtered by claim type, skipping adjusters marked unavailable |
| Q2 | At what claim amount does manager approval trigger, is it per claim or cumulative per policy, is there a second level above the manager for very large claims, and who approves when the manager is on leave? | One threshold = one simple approval process. Multiple levels or delegation = multi-step approvals, dynamic approvers, and manager hierarchy setup | Blocker | Rhea | Single threshold of $10,000 per claim, approved by the adjuster's manager, with a delegate approver when the manager is on leave |
| Q3 | What does "real-time" fraud scoring mean in seconds (under 2s, under 60s, within minutes), and must claim intake still work if the vendor API is down or slow? | A synchronous callout blocks the user and risks timeouts. Async (Queueable or Platform Event) keeps intake fast but needs retry and a "pending" status | Blocker | Marcus | Scoring runs asynchronously right after save, result within 60 seconds, intake is never blocked, failed calls retry and show "Fraud Check Pending" |
| Q4 | When a claim is flagged as suspicious, is payment automatically blocked, what score counts as "flagged", and who is allowed to release the hold? | Drives the status model, validation rule to block payment, release permission set, and audit fields | Blocker | Marcus | Score of 70 or higher sets status "SIU Review" and blocks payment until the SIU lead releases it |
| Q5 | Who is the "policyholder" in Salesforce terms: Person Account, or Account plus Contact? How many policyholders need a login, and how would they authenticate? | Person Accounts cannot be switched off later. The answer drives the data model, external license type, and sharing design | Blocker | Tom | Account plus Contact model (no Person Accounts). Policyholders get no login in Phase 1 |
| Q6 | What claim status details may policyholders see (stage, adjuster name, payment date, notes), and must internal notes and fraud information always be hidden? | Drives a separate "public status" field, field-level security, and what notification emails contain | Important | Rhea | Only stage, last-updated date, and next step. Never notes or fraud data |
| Q7 | How will garages and contractors submit estimates: partner portal login, email, or API from their own systems? How many partners and how many estimates per month? | Portal = external licenses plus Experience Cloud setup. API = custom Apex REST and authentication design. Email = Email Services plus parsing | Blocker | Leila | One secure inbound REST endpoint for partner systems, about 50 partners, no portal in Phase 1 |
| Q8 | Which records must be kept 7 years (claims, notes, documents, approvals, payments, field history), and may closed claims older than 2 years be archived outside Salesforce? | Field history has retention limits. Archiving needs Big Objects or external storage. All of it affects storage cost and design | Blocker | Anika | Claims, approvals, payments, and audit trail kept 7 years after closure. Closed claims older than 2 years archived externally |
| Q9 | How many new claims per year and on a peak day, how many attachments per claim, and what is the Salesforce storage allowance? | Drives batch sizes, indexing, large-data-volume design, and storage cost | Important | Greg | About 60,000 claims per year, peak day 3 times the average, 5 attachments per claim |
| Q10 | How many open claims are in the Excel sheets, is the claim number unique across all regional sheets, and what does "mostly clean" mean (which fields are missing, duplicated, or inconsistent)? | Decides the External ID (single field or composite), cleansing effort, and migration approach | Blocker | Dan | About 3,000 open claims. Claim number unique only per region, so use Region + Number as External ID. Client sends a 200-row sample before Sprint 8 |
| Q11 | How many regions are there, is a claim owned by the policyholder's region, loss location, or adjuster's office, and who besides Dan can see all regions? | Decides OWD, role hierarchy versus sharing rules, and region fields on the claim | Blocker | Dan | 14 offices roll up into 4 regions. Claim region = adjuster's office region. Only Dan and the VP see all |
| Q12 | Which reports must managers build themselves, which should be fixed dashboards, and do they need to combine claims, payments, and approvals in one report? | Drives custom report types, folder sharing, dashboard running user, and training | Important | Rhea | 6 fixed dashboards, custom report types, managers can clone reports inside their region folder |
| Q13 | Which fields and processes differ between auto and home claims, and when is Home needed? | One Claim object with record types versus separate objects. Getting it wrong now means a data model rebuild later | Important | Rhea | One Claim object with an Auto record type now. Home added later as a new record type |
| Q14 | After a claim is approved or paid, who can still edit or delete it (and its payment and approval records), and who can only read? | Drives record locking, validation rules, permission sets, and audit design | Blocker | Anika | Locked after payment. Only a Claims Admin can edit, with a reason. Nobody can delete |
| Q15 | How many users by type (adjusters, managers, SIU, finance, external), and what is the license budget? | Decides license types, permission set groups, and whether an external portal is affordable | Important | Greg | About 150 internal users. External users deferred |
| Q16 | Who supports the system after go-live, during what hours, how long is hypercare, and who approves production deployments? | Drives monitoring, error alerts, documentation depth, and the rollback plan | Nice-to-know | Tom | 4 weeks of hypercare, business hours, Tom approves production deployments |
| Q17 | How will Salesforce get policy data from PolicyCore: real-time API, nightly file or batch, or manual entry? Which policy fields are needed? | Decides whether policies are copied into Salesforce (batch upsert with External ID) or looked up live (callout). Affects integration scope a lot | Blocker | Tom | Nightly batch of active policies into Salesforce, read-only, matched by Policy Number as External ID |
| Q18 | Does Salesforce issue payments, or only record the approval and send it to a finance system? Which system pays the claim? | Decides whether there is a payment integration at all, and what "paid" means in the status model | Blocker | Greg | Salesforce records the payment authorization. A finance system executes payment outside Phase 1 |
